About GrazingTrack

GrazingTrack is a free, open source rotational grazing management tool for farmers.

Built with:
• Leaflet.js — open source map library
• OpenStreetMap / Esri — free satellite tiles
• Leaflet.draw — polygon drawing
• Browser localStorage — no server needed

License: MIT — free to use, modify, and share.

All your data stays on your device. No accounts, no subscriptions, no limits.
